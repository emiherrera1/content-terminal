How it is organized

          
Market keys across the top: HOME (monitors), CRTR (creators, read like stocks), NICHE (sectors and niches, read like indexes), CORP (creator companies, read like private markets), PLAT (platforms, read like macro and rates), NEWS, HELP. Each key has its own function codes in the strip below it.

          
Load a security by typing its name and key: DOCTOR MIKE CRTR, FITNESS NICHE. Add a function to jump straight there: DOCTOR MIKE CRTR RISK. Only tracked creators load: 517 YouTube creators in Lifestyle, Technology, Business & Finance and Health & Wellness. The loaded security stays in the bar under the keys while you move between functions; list functions are filtered to it. ✕ unloads it.

          
CRTR: DES (tear sheet) · SRCH · RISK · BIZ · CORP · HDS · COMP · WTCH · CN. NICHE: DES (analysis) · NI · GP · IMAP · SURF · SKEW · RPM · FMT · COV. CORP: LADR · UCAP · DEAL · HDS · BIZ · SPON. PLAT: PLAT · RATE · RPM. HOME: WEI · MOST · ALRT · TOP · RATE.

          
Commands

          
NICHE · TREND · CREATOR [search] · ALERTS · HMAP · WATCH · TEAR <creator> · COMP [A vs B] · CORP · UNDERCAP · DEALS · INVESTORS · HDS [company or funder] · RISK · STREAMS · SPONSORS · SURF [niche] · SKEW · RATES · RPM · PLATFORM · NEWS · FORMATS [search] · COVERAGE · DES <code> · ANALYZE <niche> · HELP · CLEAR

          
!LIFE, !GAME, !FINX … one sector's attention index (! for attention; $ still works).
SORT CCOS | SI | NGP | TVE | CHG on the niche table.

          
Tab completes a command. Esc goes back HOME, unloads the security and clears the search.

          
TVE
Content supply: minutes published by tracked channels in the niche over 28 days.

          
SI
Saturation, 0 to 1: uploads per 1,000 views gained over 28 days, against the median niche (0.5).

          
NGP
Growth: views gained in the last 7 days ÷ the 7 before. 1.00 is flat.

          
CCOS
Opportunity score: 20 × NGP × (1 − SI). A flat, median-saturation niche scores 10.

          
CHG% / Trend
CCOS today vs 7 days ago. UP above +3%, DOWN below −3%, else STABLE.

          
Risk
Volatility of daily views plus the top 3 channels' share of niche views, split into thirds.

          
Index
Simple average CCOS of the sector's niches; its CHG% compares today with 7 days ago.

          
News rank
Impact (HIGH 3, MEDIUM 2, LOW 1), halving every 7 days. Tags come from Claude reading each headline; every item links to its source.

          
Dog house
Articles whose reporting is negative for creators. Sourced articles only; posts from X need the paid X API and aren't connected.

          
Formats
Refreshed from the Trending Formats sheet each morning. Performance = 60 × min(change ÷ 100%, 1) + 40 × min(outlier ÷ 10x, 1). EXCELLENT 80+, STRONG 60+, GOOD 40+, FAIR 20+, else WEAK. A change of "-" counts as 0. Filters: top 10% by performance, change 100%+, outlier 6x+, 3+ triggers.

          
Heatmap
Every niche as a tile inside its sector, sized by views over 28 days (or content supply) and colored by its 7-day change in attention (or in CCOS), green up and red down, capped at ±15% (±20% for CCOS). Click a tile to analyze that niche.

          
Watchlist
Creators ranked by Creator Viability Score within tier (Diligence-ready, Priority, Watch), with a pipeline stage on each: Watching, Contacted, In diligence, Passed, Invested. Click a creator for its tear sheet: numbers, the score's pillars against their maximums, gates, brands in their videos, packaging, niche and the closest comparables, with a note field and Print / PDF.

          
Companies
The company around each creator, from the Creator Companies sheet: stage 1 no company found, 2 payout LLC, 3 operating company, 4 holding company, 5 outside capital, 6 exited. Blank means not researched yet. Undercapitalized lists creators growing faster than their niche with no outside capital found. Deals lists financing rounds; Investors counts who backs them. Every row has a source and a confidence: Confirmed (a filing or the company), Reported (press) or Inferred.

          
Comp
Two creators or two niches side by side on the same metrics. Green marks the stronger value where higher or lower is clearly better. COMP SLEEP VS NUTRITION or pick from the lists.

          
Platform
Attention per platform (9.1), each platform's week against its own 26 weeks (9.2), the follower-weighted composite (9.3) and which platform moves first in a topic (9.4).

          
Coverage
Per niche: channels tracked in the database, anchors, channels added in the last 7 days, and what screening did with every discovered channel (kept, held for review, dropped and why).

          
Definitions
Hover any underlined column or metric name for a one-line definition. DES <code> (for example DES CCOS) opens the full entry: formula, inputs, how to read it and its handbook section. Click an underlined name to open it there too.

          
Market temp
HOT above an average CCOS of 12, WARM above 8, else COOL.
